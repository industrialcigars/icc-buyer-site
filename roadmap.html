The Roadmap

Built to scale, anchored by family

Everything needed for a multi-city chain already exists.

ICC was built to be repeatable, not just successful once. The systems, SOPs, and EOS infrastructure that run this business today weren't built for one lounge — they were built to scale. The one thing that's kept ICC a single location isn't capability. It's that the founding family is deeply, personally rooted in this specific room, with this specific community — and that closeness has made the next jump a step the family hasn't been positioned to take themselves.

A new owner starts from a different position. The tools are built. The processes are documented. The proof of execution is in the weekly scorecard. What's been missing isn't the infrastructure for growth — it's an owner who isn't emotionally anchored to a single address.

Where the immediate upside is

  • Performance standards. Room to tighten expectations across a staff-heavy structure — a new owner resets the standard on day one without the history that makes those calls hard.
  • Membership quality over quantity. Continued growth paired with more deliberate curation of fit.
  • Multi-location expansion. The 2,200 sq ft build-out playbook, systems stack, staffing model, and EOS structure are proven and repeatable — with Universal Studios and PGA of America driving destination traffic, and neighboring cities offering EDC incentives for new locations.
  • A path to $3M+ annually — membership growth, ecommerce efficiency, and recurring co-branded projects — achievable before multi-location upside is even on the table.
