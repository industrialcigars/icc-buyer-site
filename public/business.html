What actually happened to net income

Revenue grew 19% — but GAAP net income shows a loss of $456,964, almost entirely driven by a single accounting entry: $446,675 of depreciation on the Suite 600 buildout was caught up and booked all at once in December 2024, rather than spread across the prior year it was actually placed in service. It's a real, correct accounting entry — and it's non-cash and non-recurring. Strip it out and 2024 was essentially break-even (~$10K), not a $457K loss.

Reading H1 correctly

H1 is structurally the softer half of ICC's year — it always has been. In 2025, cigar revenue ran 19.8% higher in H2 than H1, and total Lightspeed revenue ran 72% higher in H2 than H1. Net income follows the same shape: Q4 has been the strongest quarter every year on record, Q3 the softest. H1 2026's $14,151 net income is not a signal the year is behind — it's the expected first half of a business that earns most of its profit in the second.

What comes with it

A fully furnished, turnkey operation

Nothing here needs to be built from scratch. The humidor, the kitchen, the bar, the HVAC, the security system — all of it transfers, all of it is already paid for and depreciating on someone else's timeline instead of a new owner's.

  • Humidor: ~600 sq ft, capacity for 844 facings, ~$100K inventory
  • Top-tier furniture throughout, refreshed December 2025 and June 2026
  • Fuselage kitchen: two freezers, Blodgett combi-oven, dishwasher, 2 ice machines; F&B/MB liquor permit
  • Control4 automation, 8 DirecTV hookups, 10 TVs, full sound system
  • Brand-new 10-ton HVAC with custom integrated air purification — suite 600's install is fully custom-engineered, described as an industry first
  • Full-lounge 4K security camera system with audio, remote-viewable
  • Retail inventory on hand: ~$80K cigars + ~$10K F&B
  • ~$750K in leasehold/build-out improvements from the expansion project
