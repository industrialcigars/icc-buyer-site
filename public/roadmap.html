The Roadmap

Built to scale, anchored by family

Built to scale

The playbook already exists

ICC was built to be repeatable, not just successful once. The systems, SOPs, and EOS infrastructure that run this business today weren't built for one lounge — they were built to scale.

The one thing that's kept ICC a single location isn't capability. It's that the founding family is deeply, personally rooted in this specific room, with this specific community — and that closeness has made the next jump a step the family hasn't been positioned to take themselves.

A new owner starts from a different position. The tools are built. The processes are documented. The proof of execution is in the weekly scorecard. What's been missing isn't the infrastructure for growth — it's an owner who isn't emotionally anchored to a single address.

Where the immediate upside is

  • Performance standards. Room to tighten expectations across a staff-heavy structure — a new owner resets the standard on day one without the history that makes those calls hard.
  • Membership quality over quantity. Continued growth paired with more deliberate curation of fit.
  • Multi-location expansion. Every system a second location would need already exists and already runs: the accountability chart defining what roles to hire, the training platform to onboard them, the purchasing and pricing engines to run inventory correctly from day one, the EOS structure to keep it all disciplined. Opening location two isn't a research project — it's running the same playbook again, with the road already paved. Add in Universal Studios and PGA of America driving new destination traffic to the area, and neighboring cities offering EDC incentives for new locations, and the opportunity isn't hypothetical.
  • A path to $3M+ annually — membership growth, ecommerce efficiency, and recurring co-branded projects — achievable before multi-location upside is even on the table.
