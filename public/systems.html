Custom platforms

Nine years of learning the tech stack the hard way produced eight purpose-built systems. Each one replaced a real, specific pain point — and each one is already built, tested, and running. This is the investment already made. A new owner isn't buying a plan to build these; they're buying the finished thing, ready to perform.

01Live

Programming OS

Runs every event start to finish — bookings, staffing, run-of-show — all in one place. Before this existed, event season meant chasing details across texts and spreadsheets, with details falling through the cracks. Now it doesn't.

02Live

ICCU — ICC Academy

AI-powered training that gets every new hire to the ICC standard without pulling a manager off the floor to babysit onboarding. Consistency, without the labor cost of teaching the same thing over and over.

03Live

Member Monthly Credit Cron

Fully automated monthly retail credit — membership status flows straight to a redemption barcode with zero manual work. What used to be a recurring administrative task now runs itself, every month, without anyone remembering to do it.

04Live

Pairing Passport

QR-based system tracking member pairings, turning a single visit into an ongoing collection with real usage data behind it. Members get a reason to keep coming back; the business gets real data on what's actually being paired and enjoyed.

05Live

Purchasing / OTB Calculator

Live POS data drives exact order quantities and vendor purchase orders — not gut feel. The difference between "we think we need more of this" and "the data says exactly how much, from which vendor, this week."

06Live

Guest Pass Program

Automated guest tracking, Slack overage alerts, and welcome emails — no manual oversight required. Keeps the guest experience polished without anyone manually policing who came in on whose pass.

07In final build

Marketing Command Center

One place to plan, launch, and track marketing, with an AI agent helping execute campaigns instead of marketing living in someone's head or scattered across a dozen disconnected tools.

08In final build

Bottle Concierge Pricing Tool

Config-driven bar pricing engine applying tiered markup rules automatically across pour and concierge pricing — consistent margin, without someone doing the math by hand on every bottle.

Operating discipline

Every week, leadership reviews roughly 30 scorecard metrics — revenue by category, margins, inventory, transactions, email engagement, membership growth — each with a named owner and a hard number to hit. 2026 year-to-date is running behind several of those targets, which is exactly the gap the calculator is built to close.

Positions, not people

The business runs on a documented accountability chart: an Integrator over three functions — Revenue, Operations, and Finance — each with its own seats below it. The structure doesn't depend on any one person showing up; it depends on the seats being filled.
