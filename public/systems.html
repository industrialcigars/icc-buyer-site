Why an agency basis. A buyer who doesn't write software would not hire one freelancer to build eight connected platforms. They would commission a single engagement, with discovery, project management and QA included. That is what the figure above prices.

For reference, the other ways to price it:

  • One experienced freelancer ($125–$175 an hour) at the same 1,275 midpoint hours: 1,275 × $150 = $191,250. That is about eight months of full-time work for one person, with no project management or QA behind it.
  • The top of the agency range, 1,590 hours at $300 an hour: $477,000.
  • Scale check: the eight platforms are made up of roughly 60,000 lines of custom code.

Operating discipline

Every week, leadership reviews roughly 30 scorecard metrics — revenue by category, margins, inventory, transactions, email engagement, membership growth — each with a named owner and a hard number to hit. 2026 year-to-date is running behind several of those targets, which is exactly the gap the calculator is built to close.

Positions, not people

The business runs on a documented accountability chart: an Integrator over three functions — Revenue, Operations, and Finance — each with its own seats below it. The structure doesn't depend on any one person showing up; it depends on the seats being filled.
