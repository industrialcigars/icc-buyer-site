Systems & Infrastructure

Built to run without you standing over it

Nine years of learning the tech stack the hard way, so a new owner doesn't have to.

Custom platforms

  • Programming OS — runs every event start to finish: bookings, staffing, run-of-show, all in one place instead of texts and spreadsheets.
  • ICCU (ICC Academy) — AI-powered training that gets every new hire to the ICC standard without pulling a manager off the floor.
  • Member Monthly Credit Cron — fully automated monthly retail credit, membership status to redemption barcode, zero manual work.
  • Pairing Passport — QR-based system tracking member pairings, turning a visit into an ongoing collection with real usage data.
  • Marketing Command Center — one place to plan, launch, and track marketing, with an AI agent to help execute.
  • Purchasing / OTB Calculator — live POS data drives exact order quantities and vendor purchase orders, not gut feel.
  • Guest Pass Program — automated guest tracking, Slack overage alerts, and welcome emails, no manual oversight required.

Licensed stack in use

Lightspeed + Lightspeed Analytics (cigar POS), Toast (F&B POS), Shopify (site, ecommerce, membership), Appstle Memberships & Loyalty, Sling (scheduling), XtraChef (F&B profit/inventory), Klaviyo (email). An ICC Membership App is currently in development.

Operating discipline

Every week, leadership reviews roughly 30 scorecard metrics — revenue by category, margins, inventory, transactions, email engagement, membership growth — each with a named owner and a hard number to hit. 2026 year-to-date is running behind several of those targets, which is exactly the gap the calculator is built to close.

Positions, not people

The business runs on a documented accountability chart: an Integrator over three functions — Revenue, Operations, and Finance — each with its own seats below it. The structure doesn't depend on any one person showing up; it depends on the seats being filled.
