The Story

Why we built it, why we're selling it

Experience a true lounge — and the family behind it.

Our Timeline

The Dream

A family's dream, six points on a hexagon

This started as a family's dream. A father and his three sons who spent years smoking cigars together — a way to check in, to catch up, to enjoy the evening. What began as a pastime became a hobby, then a deep passion, then a profession. Six points on our hexagon, six members of one family — the vision held, and it's held ever since.

Hands On

The trip that decided our future

It was the founder's mother who pushed the four of them to go further than passion — she got the father and three sons on a plane to Nicaragua to learn cigar-making directly in the soil. That trip convinced them this was their future. Years later, Alejandra, Brandon's wife, took what could have been an overly-masculine cigar lounge and turned it into something else entirely — a unique experience defined by unforgettable, room-by-room differentiation.

World's First

Welcome to the Atabey Private Lounge

COVID gave us a strange gift: time to reimagine what a private membership should be. On October 17, 2020, we reopened as the Atabey Private Lounge — partnered with Nelson Alfonso of Selected Tobacco — the first of its kind. For years we'd fought anti-smoking regulation just to open the doors in the first place; this was proof it was worth it.

Punching Holes

Out of space, into Fuselage

Three years later, out of space and out of patience, we punched through the wall into a 2,200-square-foot expansion: the Dreamer public lounge, the Parlor date-night room, and Fuselage Parlor & Kitchen — bringing ICC into the F&B industry with a full kitchen and a licensed, stocked bar.

Awarded

First-ever PCA national award

In 2024, ICC became a first-ever PCA national award winner for Best Cigar Lounge — nominated against thousands of retailers nationwide. In 2026, the Best of the Leaf award expanded to include international retailers for the first time, and ICC took the stage again. ICC is now the only winner of both the national and international level PCA awards.

"There's nothing like this." — the recognition we hear most often, unprompted, from international brand owners and travelers.

Why we're selling

You cannot be great at two things at once. ICC is proof of what focus builds — nine years of discipline, EOS-driven operations, and a business that runs like a business, not a hobby. Now our family has a next chapter in the same industry: building a Frisco-first Free Trade Zone that would turn the DFW area into a hub for cigar manufacturing and distribution — cutting shipment times from weeks to minutes. That takes everything we have. ICC's next phase of growth deserves the same kind of focus, rolled out carefully, respecting the family-like community our members have built with us. We can't give both our full attention. So we're handing the next chapter of ICC to someone who can.
